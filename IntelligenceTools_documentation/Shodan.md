# $Shodan$: INTERNET EXPOSURE INTELLIGENCE
### What it means:
This intelligence tells you what assets are exposed to the internet, what services they run, and how they are configured.

### Role in our system:
- Identifies public-facing IPs, ports, technologies
- Finds risky misconfigurations (open RDP/FTP, outdated services, etc.)
- Helps determine attack surface

Gives context like:
- Which software version is running?
- Which protocol is exposed?
- Which device is it? (router, firewall, camera, server, etc.)

Why it's important:
- Before you detect malware or vulnerabilities, you must know what is actually visible to attackers.

**Shodan = “What can attackers see?”**
